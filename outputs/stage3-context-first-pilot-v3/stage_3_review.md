## `case_0001`

**Domain:** biology  
**Source schema:** `mafalda_gold_000123`  
**Fallacy:** Hasty generalization

**Context:** In a biology class, students are learning about the effects of different substances on plant growth. The teacher presents a case study: a farmer has been using a new organic fertilizer on his tomato plants for three years. During that time, the farmer has never gotten a sunburn while working in the fields. The teacher asks the class to consider whether the fertilizer might be responsible for preventing sunburn.

**Question:** Based on the case study, what is the most reasonable conclusion about the fertilizer's effect on sunburn?

**Possible reasoning:** The farmer used the fertilizer for three years and never got a sunburn, so the fertilizer must prevent sunburn. But this is just one person, and there could be many other reasons (e.g., he always wears sunscreen, works in shade, has naturally resistant skin). Concluding from a single case that the fertilizer prevents sunburn is a hasty generalization.

**Possible misconception:** The fertilizer prevents sunburn in humans.
